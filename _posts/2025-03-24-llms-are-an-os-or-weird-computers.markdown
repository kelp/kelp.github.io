Phillip Carter on [LLMs Are Weird Computers](https://www.phillipcarter.dev/posts/llms-computers)

> If traditional computers are precision machines that struggle with fuzziness, LLMs are the 
> inverse: fuzzy machines that struggle with precision (unless calling out to a traditional
> computer to do a precision task).

I've been thinking about this since I stumbled on it, and I think it's a very astute observation
about the nature of LLMs. And it's early days, we're just getting started figuring out how to
use them effectively.

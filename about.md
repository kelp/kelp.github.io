I'm Travis Cole. I'm SVP of Engineering at
[PlanetScale](https://planetscale.com/), where we build high-performance
databases for MySQL and Postgres.

Previously, I led Cloud Infrastructure at Confluent, the SRE team at
Segment, and Platform Engineering Infrastructure at Square.

Elsewhere you might find me skiing, trail running, or maybe trying to
thru-hike (or section hike) some of the (shorter) long distance trails
such as the John Muir Trail and the Tahoe Rim Trail.

## About This Site

This site was built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/), a static site
generator written in Ruby. The minimalist theme was created with
[Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code), inspired by
[Perfect Motherfucking Website](https://perfectmotherfuckingwebsite.com/).
I wanted a site that focuses on readability and content without
unnecessary styling or JavaScript - just pure HTML and CSS the way the
web was meant to be.

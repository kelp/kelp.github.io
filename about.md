I'm Travis Cole and I lead the Infrastructure team at PlanetScale, helping
to make MySQL scale horizontally for lots of big applications.

Previously, I worked at Confluent leading Cloud Infrastructure, at Segment
leading the SRE team, and at Square leading Platform Engineering Infrastructure.

Elsewhere you might find me skiing, trail running, or maybe trying to
thru-hike (or section hike) some of the (shorter) long distance trails
such as the John Muir Trail and the Tahoe Rim Trail.

## About This Site

This site was built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/), a static site generator written in Ruby. The minimalist theme was created with [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code), inspired by [Perfect Motherfucking Website](https://perfectmotherfuckingwebsite.com/). I wanted a site that focuses on readability and content without unnecessary styling or JavaScript - just pure HTML and CSS the way the web was meant to be.
